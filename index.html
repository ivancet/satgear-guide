    Before you pay: questions for the seller

      Need IP input (UDP / RTP)? On the MCX7000 that's VI-01 (GbE TSoIP with SMPTE 2022 FEC), which only works with a video package: VP-01 to transmit, or VP-02 for receive only. Only pay for VI-02 and the HS-05 ASI board if you really need ASI. On these units ASI uses slot 3, so you lose the third demodulator.

      How these numbers are made

      Listings

      Every priced row was opened and read on the date shown. These are asking prices; dealers usually take less, and eBay "or best offer" listings sell below the ask. USD is converted at the ECB reference rate on that date. Shipping, VAT and import duties are not included.

      Estimates

      The MCX7000 and MDM6100 show no public price anywhere we looked: dealers only quote. Their ranges are estimates built from the priced siblings on the same hardware generation, with the reasoning shown on each card. Treat them as a starting point for negotiation, not as market data.

      Why licences matter more than the box

      Newtec units are sold "pay-as-you-grow": the same chassis can be a single 72 MBaud demod or a 3 × 133 MBaud gateway, depending on licence keys tied to the serial number. A unit with the wrong licences may cost more to upgrade than buying a better-licensed one.
